## I am

I'm **Augustus Nguyen**, based in Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. Mathematics took me through middle school and high school. Engineering came later, and it uses a different test for being right. In mathematics, a proof that works in a billion cases and fails in one is dead. In engineering, getting more than half of it right is already valuable. I stumbled over that difference for a long time before I got used to it.

## I work

I'm a software engineer, and I focus on backend and DevOps. I build backend services in Go, and I run them on Kubernetes with infrastructure as code, CI/CD, and observability.

Outside work, I learn by building things from scratch. On [GitHub](https://github.com/0x48core) I'm writing [a Kafka-like commit log](https://github.com/0x48core/logcore) to understand storage engines and event-driven systems. I also keep hands-on repositories for [distributed systems patterns](https://github.com/0x48core/designing-distributed-systems-hands-on), [observability](https://github.com/0x48core/o11y-platform) (metrics, logs, traces and alerts), [infrastructure as code](https://github.com/0x48core/iac-hands-on), [Kubernetes](https://github.com/0x48core/k8s-hands-on), and [latency in Go](https://github.com/0x48core/go-latency).

## I write

I love it when math and programming meet. Writing those ideas down and sharing them is what sharpens my mind and nurtures my passion. I find happiness not only in the final results but also in the process of striving.

Most of what I publish is data structures, algorithms, and mathematics. Those posts live on the [Blogs](/blogs/) page.
